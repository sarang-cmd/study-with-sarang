What it does

Less going on. More getting done.

The app opens in Essentials: three pages, one timer, a few icons. Everything else is still there, one switch away in Settings, Advanced.

Goal first

Press Start and it asks what the block is for and how long you will take. When the block ends it asks if you got there and how focused you felt, then what to do next.

Breaks that help

Rest ideas that actually rest you: look far away, stretch, water, a short walk, breathing. Never a scroll. You decide before the break, not during it.

Study that sticks

Blank-page recall, practice questions and spaced reviews at 1, 3 and 7 days, built on the research that testing yourself beats rereading.

Sound made on your device

Twelve base environments and layers, generated live. No recordings, no streaming, no brainwave claims. Keep the volume comfortable.

Honest progress

Weekly bars, a year heatmap, streaks, goal days and 91 achievements, all stored on your device. Visiting is not studying; you decide what counts.

Say it, do it

"Flow, pause my music." Hold a key and speak: music in other apps, timers, goals, to-dos, reviews, pages. Pick the voice engine that answers you.

Real notifications

Windows notifications for blocks, breaks and reviews, with a test button that tells you exactly what happened.

Yours to tune

Nothing is forced. Subjects and exam seasons for the IB, GCSE, A Level, AP and more. Your own icon, themes, shortcuts and a mini bar that stays on top.

Private by design

No account is needed. Optional sync uses your own Supabase project. API keys stay on your device. Discord presence is optional.

A look inside

Built around the session.

Before you start

A short question, not a form. Name the goal, pick how you will study it, choose the minutes. Or press Just start.

When it ends

Did you reach the goal? How focused was it? Add reviews for tomorrow and the days after, then take a break, carry on or stop.

Rest on purpose

The break screen shows one gentle idea at a time and reminds you to name your next step before you return.

See where the hours go

Stats, a year of activity and your subjects. Essentials keeps a short weekly summary; switch on everything for the full dashboard.

The dark side of the studio

Light and dark, a zen view and a calm screen for breathing. Same grayscale design everywhere.

Questions

Good to know.

Windows says the app is not recognized. Is it safe?

The installer is signed with a certificate I made myself, because commercial certificates cost money and a new program has no reputation yet. Windows SmartScreen therefore asks once: choose More info, then Run anyway. You can compare the SHA-256 on the release page with Get-FileHash in PowerShell. The full source is in the repository.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No. There are no ads and no tracking. Cloud sync, AI helpers and voice engines are optional and use your own accounts and keys, which stay on your device.

Where is my data?

On your device, in the browser's storage or the app's data folder. Export and import are in Settings, Your data.

Can I change what the app shows?

Yes. The default is Essentials. Open Settings, Advanced and switch on Show everything to bring back the full set: all pages, the assistant, automations, the study room and more.

How does it update?

The desktop app checks the GitHub releases page and asks before installing anything. Every update is checked against its SHA-256 first. You can switch the check off.
